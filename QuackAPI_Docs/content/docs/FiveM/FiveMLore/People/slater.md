### Slater, Just Slater it’s a mononym like Prince or Cher, not Mr Slater, or Slater Smith as his main passport says, just Slater  

He is the Pyromaniac fire chief of Los Santos, thriving in Chaos and the heat of, well fire. Slater likes anything that moves fast, goes boom or flies (and preferably all three at once).  

If something is going boom or otherwise on fire, Slater is probably around, either having caused it, to enjoy the view or to clean up the mess afterwards or again maybe all three.
Slater is an expert pilot and can fly pretty much anything that is an afront to God and physics, but you probably don’t want to be in a fast car with him if he is driving, or quite frankly anywhere in the same postcode.  

Slater tries to fix issues with words before having to resort to violence, very rarely starting a fight himself. But when attacked or provoked, well let’s just say a city block is not that hard to make go away. Putting it simply, expect any attack to be parried with extreme amounts of violence and firepower.  

---  

Not much is known about Slaters early life, but it is known sometime in early adulthood he ended up working for the CIA, being known as an expert marksmen and pilot, but also being banned from the driving range due to the number of totalled cars.  
After a botched CIA Drug operation, Slater found himself washed up on a beach in a city known as (Server 1). Presumed KIA By the Agency Slater went on a hunt for information as too who sold the team out and who threw him overboard.  

in (Server 1) Slater found an old colleague known as Tom, who some evidence pointed to being the cause of the botched operation and as a result Slater successfully framed him for a shop robbery and left him in (Server 1)  
After a couple of botched bank robberies in the aim to find information on “The Reaper” Slater joined the fire department quickly rising through the ranks and then eventually becoming fire Chief in (Server 2)  

With his Nose for interesting things, and role of not a cop, Slater normally can gain some level of trust with the criminal world and gain connections within it. Along with his ability to source almost anything at a wholesale level, he can be an asset to anyone he likes.  

Upon Returning to (Server 2) Slater began consolidating funds from various black bank accounts and bureaucratic fuckery, Slater was able to retake control of Prometheus heavy industries and purchased the catcafe from the owners that mysteriously found themselves in vats of acid.  

### Drink Of Choice  

Slaters drink of choice, is somewhat egotistically, the "Flaming Slater"  

- One Large Shot of Kraken Rum, Can be substituted for Cherry Kraken or Mount Gay Rum  
- Cola, One can to about a 60ml (1.6oz) of Rum  
- One Large Whiskey esc Ice Cube  - this should be enough to be popping its head just above the rum and cola when poured  
- A Dash of Gunpowder  
- Opt, Dash of lemon juice  

> Preperation  

- Place Ice Cub in Whiskey glass and slowly pour the Rum over it  
- Slowly Pour the Cola Over the ice cub and (opt) Add the dash of lemon juice  
- Carefully place or pour the gun powder onto the top of the ice cube  
- Once Ready to serve light the gun powder for a nice flash, if you have done this right the rum in the drink should briefly ignite  

This drink is great to start the night off
